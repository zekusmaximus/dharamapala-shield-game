# Player task card · Dharmapala Shield

You are protecting a network in a tower-defense game. Please play as you
normally would, starting a new game and seeing how far you can get through the
first five waves. You can use any information and controls the game provides.
Decide for yourself when to start each wave, whether to change speed, and whether
to retry after a loss. There is no time limit and no required build.

At the end, we will ask what you noticed, what informed your choices, and whether
you would want to play again. If something is unclear, you can say so. You may
stop at any time.
