## One behavior, no schedule retune

The existing Federal Agent gains **shield brace**, a one-use self shield. It is
already present in the authored wave-3/4 escort packets; it does not protect
neighbors. Reusing the finite-shield impact/accounting path is smaller than
adding an ally targeting/aura or a second disable system. No second behavior is
needed to demonstrate distinct responses, and no inert legacy label returns.

The exact parsed-content differences from M3.3 are:

- `version`: 1.3.0 → **1.4.0**.
- `enemies.federalAgent.brace`: `{windupMs:3000, durationMs:4000, healthRatio:0.6}`.

**No first-five content/schedule changes are necessary** beyond this enemy rule.
All 20 wave definitions, offsets, intervals, counts, HP multipliers and rewards
are byte-unchanged. The map, tower/enemy base stats, prices, upgrades, explicit
specializations, armor/resists, Dharma/Bandwidth income, retired Anonymity and
boss abilities remain unchanged. Existing later Agent waves and reinforcements
inherit the rule; their schedules are not redesigned. M3.1's shared compiler,
preview windows, overlap/arrival-gap caveats, 1×/2× timing and deliberate starts
are preserved.

[The content contract](../../CONTENT_SCHEMA.md#m34-agent-shield-brace-balance-140-schema-v4)
specifies targeting/range, exact fixed-tick boundaries, damage/status stacking,
expiry, death/terminal cleanup and compatibility. In brief: warn three simulated
seconds from spawn; grant `round(maxHealth × 0.6)` shield once; discard its
remainder four seconds later. Shield never refills or regenerates. Damage after
armor/resistance removes shield then health; all attack types and existing
slow/vulnerability still work. Death/leak removes pending state; terminal freezes
retained entities and further behavior updates; restart clears them. An early
kill avoids activation, sustained hits can break capacity, or later coverage can
fight after expiry. These are build-phase preparations, not new in-wave actions.

Canvas uses compact **! warning / S shield / × broken / ✓ spent** glyphs with a
visible legend, dashed warning ring and active shield ring/bar. The initial long

symbols were checked again in portrait and desktop. Full per-Agent semantic
Combat status gives timing, current/max shield and no-refill/expiry text. Agent
roster inspection explains the effect and multiple responses before Start Wave.
Feedback remains static at both motion preferences and uses the existing
keyboard-focusable non-live status panel. Dense status badges can still overlap;
semantic text is available independently. Captures [warning portrait](warning-portrait.png)
and [active desktop](active-desktop.png) use a **skipped-wave presentation

with `PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH=/workspace/.playwright-browsers node

## Earned production evidence

[counterplay.json](counterplay.json), generated by
`tools/balance/counterplay.mjs`, records 30 fresh policy/content/difficulty
first-five slices, each repeated identically: expansion, upgrades, control,
support and saving, under M3.3/current Easy/Normal/Hard. They buy the common
opening with default funds/lives and fight every wave in order. All slices
complete; Easy/Normal and four Hard plans finish at 20 lives. Hard saving retains
the same five wave-3 leaks / 15 lives as M3.3. This is not a human difficulty
or optimality result.

The artifact separately records **60 earned wave-3 purchase options** (10 responses
× 3 difficulties × 2 content versions), each repeated. 48 are affordable and
run combat; 12 blocked level-3 control choices remain recorded and are not funded.
The common opening-only checkpoint is earned through production waves 1–2,
carrying resources, lives, cooldowns and reports forward. No grants, wave skips,
entity edits, direct hits, synthetic kills or clears produce these outcomes.
Whole option costs are checked before purchasing. Exact tower positions, stats,
coverage samples, costs/banks, per-Agent phase observations, resolution time,
M2 reports and all ledgers are retained.

Normal's earned checkpoint has **225 Dharma / 96 Bandwidth**. Three more base
Firewalls cost 90/0 at each of early, central and late positions. Early expansion
kills one Agent during warning and two during the active shield, removes 132
shield health and clears in **12.633 s**. Late expansion kills all three after
expiry, removes **zero** shield health and clears in **18.067 s**. Both retain
20 lives. Central expansion clears in 14.883 s and removes 66 shield health.
The same placements before M3.4 clear in 12.483 / 18.067 / 14.117 s respectively.
Thus bypassing the window and fighting through it both occur in production;
this is not a claim that late placement is generally faster or best.

A level-2 Firewall upgrade costs 59/0 and clears Normal in 16.083 s. Focus and
Coverage level-3 upgrades each cost 173/0 from the installed level 1; both clear,
in 15.433 / 16.083 s. Encryption level 2 costs 98/68 and clears in 18.717 s;
its level-3 branches cost 288/201 beyond the installed base and are unavailable
here. No automatic specialization or imaginary Bandwidth makes them accessible.
On Hard, equal-cost early and late expansion both retain 20 lives; saving
finishes at 15, Firewall level 2 at 19 and Encryption level 2 at 18. Focus and
Coverage both retain 20. These are placement/purchase-specific comparisons, not
proof one branch or tower is universally better. The independent Firewall-only
fresh campaigns also clear all difficulties, so these measured successes do not
require control or support.

All combat uses production commands, clock, targeting, projectiles, movement,
armor/statuses, shields, EMP/reinforcements, resolution, rewards and accounting.
The code contains separate skipped-wave/direct-impact/boundary fixtures for rule
verification; they are not substituted for fresh or earned evidence above.
M3.2's funded/skipped-wave branch experiment and M3.3's earned-resource experiment
remain pinned to their immutable historical content and regenerate unchanged.

still loses Hard on wave 20 and weak placement still loses wave 3 everywhere.
Timings and shield/health/status activity change. Full tables preserve differences
against the much earlier corrected M1.4/M2.4 reports without attributing the whole
history to brace. Persistent cooldowns, timing and subsequent purchases can
propagate through later unchanged content; no single late-wave causal path is
inferred.

Shield removal counts absorbed damage after armor/resistance, **not** capacity
granted or discarded on expiry. Health removal is capped and includes existing
vulnerability without causal allocation. Status applications include refreshes
and lethal hits; they are not time saved or prevented leaks. Aura-present shots
may overlap/cap and are not extra shots caused by support. Sampled coverage is
geometry, not acquisition/cooldown/hit evidence. Deterministic repeats establish
reproducibility; difficulties share a map/roster and are not independent player
trials. No human engagement, difficulty or build-diversity conclusion follows.

## Saves, focused checks and limits

Balance **1.4.0**, wrapper/state schema **v4**, storage discovery slot
`dharmapala-shield/save/v2`, and unchanged independent preferences are explicit.
New Agent `brace` stores absolute activation/expiry deadlines plus a one-use
activation boolean; existing shield fields store remaining capacity. Full
candidate validation checks deadline derivation, phase/activation consistency,
shield capacity/regen, zero shield outside its window and absence on other types
before session swap. There is no v3 → v4 conversion, including build/terminal
runs, and no pre-v2 economy conversion. Original bytes are retained, writes are
blocked, export is offered and reset is explicit. Preferences/latest recovery
archive survive reset; export first for durable retention.

runtime and content**: Encryption at (210,210), wave 1, 120 production steps,
pause. It is a recovery fixture, not a completed-combat result. Browser checks
export exact bytes and reset with preferences preserved. Integration checks
also reject M3.3 building, wave, paused and natural-defeat saves.

Focused rules cover content rejection, one-use grant/break/expiry, skipped windows,
exact boundaries and dead enemies. Production collision tests check activation
and expiry **before** impacts on the boundary tick; shield overflow/status and
capped accounting remain tested. No unused expiry receives damage credit.
Pending saves reject malformed/foreign states. Both speeds check the ticks before
and after deadlines across pause/JSON reload. Earned forks compare uninterrupted
versus multiple paused checkpoints through remaining combat and full reports.
Existing M1/M2 terminal, preferences, aura/passive, report, speed, scheduling and
specialization regressions remain.

Browser checks use ordinary clicks and actual Tab/Enter, including portrait
inspection, live warning/active/spent states, pause/reload/Continue at both speeds

both motion preferences. This is **automated headless Chromium/axe**; canvas
pixels are excluded from axe. It is not physical-device, screen-reader,
